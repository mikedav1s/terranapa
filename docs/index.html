    Songs to know

      Ear mode. The music is hidden. Play it, sing it back, then find it on the neck. Use Step through to check yourself note by note.

      Listening mode

      The app listens through your microphone and checks each note you play. Wear headphones, or turn Guide notes off, so it only hears your guitar.

      Timing calibration

      If you are playing in time but notes score as late, raise this. Bluetooth headphones and speakers often need 150 ms or more.

      Your lick

      Type a phrase in the app's notation, or paste ASCII tab from any transcription and convert it. It is saved in this browser and gets every practice tool: playback, tempo, looping and listening mode.

      Paste ASCII tab

      ASCII tab does not record rhythm, so every note starts with the same length. Step-through mode ignores rhythm entirely; edit the durations afterwards if you want to play along in time.

      Scales & modes

      Outlined notes are quarter tones: fret the note shown and bend it up slightly, about half the distance to the next fret. Tap any note on the neck to hear it.
